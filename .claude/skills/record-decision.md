# Skill: Record Architecture Decision

## When to Use
When making a significant design choice — technology, pattern, approach, or trade-off.

## Instructions
1. Read existing decisions in `docs/decisions/` to get the next number
2. Create `docs/decisions/NNN-short-title.md` with these sections:
   - **Date**: When the decision was made
   - **Status**: Accepted / Superseded / Deprecated
   - **Context**: What problem or question prompted this decision
   - **Decision**: What we decided and why
   - **Consequences**: Positive, negative, and future implications
   - **References**: Links to research, docs, or prior art (if applicable)
